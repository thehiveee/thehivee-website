thehivee is a UK-based digital marketing agency providing Google Ads management and website design services to HVAC businesses in the United States. If you have any questions about this policy, you can reach us at enquiry@thehivee.co.

3. How We Use Your Information

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable, we process personal data on the basis of your consent (for example, submitting a contact form) or our legitimate interest in responding to business inquiries.

We do not sell your personal information. We may share necessary information with:

We do not share your information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

6. Cookies

This website may use cookies and similar technologies for analytics and to improve your browsing experience. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, though some site features may not function as intended.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us at enquiry@thehivee.co.

9. International Data Transfers

As a UK-based business serving clients in the United States, information you provide may be processed in the UK, the US, or other locations where our service providers operate.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

11. Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be directed to enquiry@thehivee.co.
